How the calculation works

We treat the milk as a single uniform temperature (lumped thermal mass) that loses heat exponentially toward the bath. Three thermal resistances sit in series between the milk and the cooling water.

Newton's Law of Cooling

The energy balance on the milk:

ρ · V · c_p · dT/dt = −U · A · (T − T_env)

This ODE has the closed-form solution:

T(t) = T_env + (T₀ − T_env) · exp(−t / τ)

Time to reach a target temperature

Inverting the exponential gives the formula used for every result on this page:

t* = τ · ln[ (T₀ − T_env) / (T_target − T_env) ]

Time constant τ

τ = ρ · V · c_p / (U · A)

ρ · V · c_p is the thermal mass of the milk. U · A is the total thermal conductance between milk and bath.

Overall heat-transfer coefficient U

Three resistances in series — inner convection (milk → wall), wall conduction, outer convection (wall → bath water):

1/U = 1/h_i + d/k + 1/h_o

Effective surface area A (cylindrical bottle, radius r)

A_total = 2V/r + 2πr² (sides + bottom cap — submerge) A_sides = 2V/r (sides only — running tap or sitting on counter)

Running tap: bottle is lopsided, bottom never contacts the flow. Sitting: bottom rests on the counter, only the sides exchange heat with air. Both lose ≈ 35 % of the surface area compared to full submersion.

For running tap the higher h_o (800 vs 300 W m⁻² K⁻¹) only partially compensates the area loss — net U·A is still ≈ 27 % lower than a still bath. For sitting (air), h_o drops to just 8 W m⁻² K⁻¹ — expect 2–3 hours.

Why 70 °C?

The WHO guidelines on powdered formula preparation recommend using water at a minimum of 70 °C to kill Cronobacter sakazakii and other heat-sensitive pathogens. At ≈ 70 °C the first visible small bubbles appear in heating water — dissolved gases nucleating on the vessel surface, not true steam boiling.

Limitations

The lumped model assumes the milk is well-mixed (uniform temperature). In practice a small gradient exists; real cool-down times may differ by ±10–20 %. The bottle neck, teat and cap are ignored — they add thermal resistance and reduce the effective area slightly, so actual times will tend to be a little longer than shown.
